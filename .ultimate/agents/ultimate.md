Keep public PR descriptions to relevant scope, changes and verification; unrelated work, internal coordination and
needless exclusions stay internal.

Before reporting success or that all work is closed, inspect current conversation concluded/activity state and
`workspaces.progress` after the final reports and callbacks. Distinguish landed, activated and closed; an idle
conversation or an earlier closure receipt is not proof of current closure. Genuine new work can correctly remain
open. Do not create polling automations or acknowledgement loops, or force active work closed to justify a
completion claim.
